Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

At Vitara Healthcare, your privacy and the security of your medical and personal information are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our website, patient portal, or clinical services:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and mailing address.
  • Health and Medical Information: Medical history, diagnoses, treatment plans, prescriptions, and lab results, strictly in accordance with HIPAA regulations.
  • Payment Information: Insurance details, billing address, and payment card details (processed securely via third-party gateways).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and usage metrics when you interact with our digital platforms.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to provide and improve our healthcare services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Provide medical consultations, diagnoses, and treatments.
  • Process payments and coordinate with your insurance provider.
  • Communicate with you regarding appointments, test results, and administrative updates.
  • Improve the functionality and security of our Patient Portal and Telemedicine platform.

3. Data Security and HIPAA Compliance

We implement stringent technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect your Protected Health Information (PHI). All data transmitted through our portal is encrypted end-to-end. We are fully compliant with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and conduct regular security audits to ensure your data remains secure.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal or medical information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other healthcare providers for the purpose of your treatment (e.g., specialists, pharmacies).
  • With your health insurance plan for billing and claims processing.
  • With authorized third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform (under strict Business Associate Agreements).
  • When required by law or to respond to legal processes.

5. Your Rights

You have specific rights regarding your personal and health information:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of your medical records.
  • The right to request corrections to any inaccurate information.
  • The right to request a restriction on how we use or share your data.
  • The right to be notified in the event of a breach of unsecured PHI.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your health information, please contact our Privacy Officer:
