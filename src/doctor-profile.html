About Dr. Chen

Dr. Michael Chen is a board-certified electrophysiologist and cardiologist with over 15 years of experience in diagnosing and treating complex heart arrhythmias. He specializes in advanced ablation techniques for atrial fibrillation (AFib) and ventricular tachycardia, as well as the implantation of cardiac devices such as pacemakers and defibrillators.

Before joining Vitara Healthcare, Dr. Chen served as the Director of Electrophysiology at Mount Sinai Hospital. He is dedicated to providing personalized, patient-centered care, taking the time to explain complex conditions and treatment options in an accessible manner.
