How Telemedicine Works

A seamless digital experience designed around your comfort and privacy.

1

Book Your Slot

Select "Telehealth" as your visit type when booking online. Choose a time that works for you.

2

Complete Pre-check

Log into the Patient Portal to fill out any necessary intake forms and verify your insurance.

3

Join the Call

Click the secure link in your email or portal to join the virtual waiting room. Your doctor will connect shortly.

4

Follow-up & Meds

Prescriptions are sent instantly to your pharmacy. A visit summary will be available in your portal.
